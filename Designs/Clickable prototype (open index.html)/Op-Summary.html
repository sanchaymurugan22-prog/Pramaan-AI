Ransomware attack on hospital networks
30 September 2026 · 1 page · 90-second read

What happened

A ransomware group has disrupted 42 hospitals in five states since 22 September by entering through remote-access gateways that missed the September patch.

Why it matters

Patient-record and billing systems are down in affected hospitals. Backups on the same network were also locked, which slows recovery.

What we are doing

An advisory has gone to hospital IT teams with three actions: patch gateways, move backups offline and test a restore.

Decision needed

Approve a public awareness post and ask state health departments to confirm patching by Friday.
